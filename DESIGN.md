# Design

## The Burlared theatre noir

Customer booking is an **Experience** surface: a dark theatre house, deep oxblood curtain field, ivory ticket paper and quiet brass-like warm details. The event title uses Pridi for a dramatic Thai display voice; Noto Sans Thai carries forms, synopsis and operational text for legibility.

The first viewport gives the event name and synopsis room to breathe before the booking flow begins. The customer’s action surface remains light, tactile and high-contrast. Selection, payment and confirmation use the same ticket-paper treatment.

Admin is an **Operate** surface sharing the red-curtain identity, but its information remains dense and scan-friendly: dark stage background, ivory records, red decisions and restrained green/amber/red status states. No booking or inventory rule is represented only by colour.

## Responsive rules

Customer stays single-column with large touch targets. Admin metric cards become two columns on a phone; controls remain horizontally actionable without hiding staff workflows.
